בדיוק זה — UI review

A static snapshot of every major screen and state of the “בדיוק זה” shopping-list app (Hebrew, right-to-left), captured directly from the running app with the current design, text and colors. Nothing here is redesigned. Click any screenshot to open it full size.

1The list — הרשימה (person composing the list)

1.1 List with items

Main composer screen with five items in the default list “סופר”. Shows the list switcher, a search field with a camera/scan button, items grouped by department, an urgent item pinned on top with a shopper note, quantity stepper, alternatives and “added by” info. At the bottom are the two actions “שליחה לקונה” (send to shopper) and “אני יוצא לקנות” (I'm going shopping).

1.2 Searching to add

Typing “חלב” in the search field shows matching catalog products with photo, size tag and a + button.

1.3 Shopper note sheet

Bottom sheet for adding a note to an item for the shopper, with an urgent checkbox.

1.4 New list

Creating an additional list, with suggested names (סופר, פארם, שוק, שבת, מכולת).

1.5 Edit list

The ⋯ menu on the active list: rename or delete it.

1.6 Empty list

Empty state when the active list has no items.

2Sharing and notifications

2.1 Who's on the list

Sheet opened from the header pill: members with presence, plus instructions for inviting a partner.

2.2 Notifications banner

Notices sent from the shopper: a list was sent (with “למה להביא” shortcut), finished shopping with missing items, and finished with everything found. Collapsed notices expand on tap.

3Shopping — מה להביא (the shopper at the store)

3.1 Shopping view

Large product cards with photo and exact size so the shopper finds the exact product. Progress bar on top, “לקחתי” (took it) and “לא מצאתי” (not found) per item, and “סיימתי את הקניות” at the bottom.

3.2 In progress: taken + not found

Bananas marked as taken (dimmed, “החזר לרשימה” to undo). Cheese marked not found, so an alternative from the same category is offered (“לקחתי את זה”).

3.3 Finish confirmation

Tapping finish while items remain opens “בטוח שסיימת?” with the unmarked items and an explanation that the list creator will be notified.

3.4 After finishing

The list closes; the shopping tab shows its empty state.

4Catalog — המוצרים שלנו (17,359 Israeli products)

4.1 Catalog grid

Department filter, “+ מוצר חדש”, search, diet filters (all / gluten-free / vegan) and product cards with photo, size tag and add button.

4.2 Pagination

40 products per page with numbered pager at the bottom.

4.3 Gluten-free filter

Diet chip active; cards show a “ללא גלוטן” badge.

4.4 Catalog search

Searching “ויטמין D” (pharmacy/vitamins department).

5Products, photos and scanning

5.1 Product editor

Tapping a product opens its editor: photo (camera/upload, gallery, paste, Google image search), name, brand, size, unit, package type, department, alternatives search with suggestions, note, and the green primary action “הוספה לרשימה”.

5.2 New product

Same form, empty, for adding a product that is not in the catalog.

5.3 Barcode scan result

After photographing a package, a recognized barcode shows the exact matching product with “הוספה לרשימה”.

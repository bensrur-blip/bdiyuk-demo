A static snapshot of every major screen and state of the “בדיוק זה” shopping-list app (Hebrew, right-to-left), captured directly from the running app with the current design, text and colors. Nothing here is redesigned. Click any screenshot to open it full size.

1The list — הרשימה (composing the list)

1.1 List (full screen)

Main screen. The search field “מה צריך לקנות?” (with camera/barcode scan) is the fastest way to add. Below it, “הוספה מהירה” shows the household’s ⭐ favourites and frequently bought products for one-tap adding. Each row shows only photo, name, size and quantity (trash icon at quantity 1); urgent items are grouped on top. Two fixed actions sit above the tab bar: “שלח למי שקונה” (send to the shopper) and “אני עושה את הקניות” (I'm doing the shopping).

1.2 Row options (⋯)

Everything else is tucked behind “⋯”: save as favourite, urgent, note for the shopper, details & alternative, remove, plus the approved alternative and who added the item.

1.3 Searching to add

Typing “חלב” ranks favourites (★) and frequently bought products first. The results also offer “מוצר חדש” and “עיון בכל המוצרים” (browse all products).

1.4 Note for the shopper

Bottom sheet for a per-item note, with an urgent checkbox.

1.5 New list

Creating an additional list with suggested names.

1.6 Edit list

Rename or delete the active list.

1.7 Empty list

Empty state for a new household (no favourites yet): an explanation of the ⭐ and a paste-a-list shortcut.

2Sharing and notifications

2.1 Who's on the list

Members with presence and how to invite a partner.

2.2 Notifications banner

A list was sent to you (with a shortcut to the shopping screen), finished with missing items, finished with everything found.

3Shopping — מה להביא (the flagship, used in the store)

3.1 Shopping view (full screen)

App colours around bright white cards for supermarket light. One large card per product: big photo, large name, size tag, quantity and note. Huge “✓ לקחתי” / “✗ לא מצאתי” buttons (icon + text, not colour only). Sticky progress bar on top, “סיימתי את הקניות” fixed at the bottom.

3.2 Not found → approved alternative

After “לא מצאתי” the card shows “✗ לא נמצא במדף” and the alternative the list owner approved in advance (“החלופה שאושרה מראש”) with a large photo and “לקחתי את החלופה”. Without an approved alternative a same-category suggestion is shown instead. “בעצם מצאתי אותו” undoes.

3.3 In progress (full screen)

Taken items move to a compact “בעגלה ✓” section at the bottom with an undo button.

3.4 Finish confirmation

“בטוח שסיימת?” lists unmarked items; the list creator is notified about what was missing.

3.5 After finishing

The list closes and the shopping screen is empty.

4Favourites — קבועים (what the household always buys)

4.1 Favourites tab (full screen)

Shared ⭐ favourites with one-tap add (or quantity when already in the list), “add all missing favourites”, “מהקנייה הקודמת” (last trip: remove individual products with ✕, remove the whole card, or add them all again) and “קניתם לאחרונה” (recently bought, removable). Entry point to search all products.

5All products — כל המוצרים (17,359 Israeli products)

5.1 All products

Reached from search or the favourites tab (with a back button). Department filter, search, diet filters, cards with photo, size, + and ★.

5.2 Pagination

40 products per page.

5.3 Gluten-free filter

Diet chip active; cards show the “ללא גלוטן” badge.

5.4 Search

“ויטמין D” in the pharmacy/vitamins department.

6Product editor, photos and scanning

6.1 Product editor (simple)

Only what a family member needs: photo, name, quantity in the list, alternative and note, plus ★ favourite. Primary green action “הוספה לרשימה” / “עדכון ברשימה”.

6.2 “פרטים נוספים” (more details)

Brand, size, package type, department, gallery, Google image search, paste and delete are collapsed here.

6.3 New product

For a product not in the catalog; size stays visible because exact size is the core of the product.

6.4 Barcode scan result

A recognised barcode shows the exact matching product.
